Cause and repair
The predicted cause required the parent to remain a regular file while validating
the child, despite having accepted its pending deletion. The public-runner fixture
contradicted neither that premise nor the predicted lost child: both runners showed
the same failure. A successful pre-fix child would have contradicted the finding.
The response oneshot releases only after preflight and a parent-byte assertion;
there are no sleeps. The real gateway sorts parent deletion before child creation
in crates/prod-code-gateway/src/runner/snapshot.rs:321 (inspected, unchanged).

crates/prod-code-mcp/src/exec/writeback.rs:224. read_in_batch at line 250 consults
captured ancestor identities: only a previously validated ordinary-file deletion
in this batch makes its children absent in the pending filesystem. It does not
turn arbitrary ENOTDIR into absence. A captured ancestor without an accepted
deletion blocks its children even if that ancestor was locally deleted or replaced
with an empty directory. Unknown/cache prefix guards and leaf identity comparison
remain in their original control flow. Symlink ancestors do not receive the exemption.
